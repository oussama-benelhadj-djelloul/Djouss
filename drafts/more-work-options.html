“More work” — closed-state options

Click each one to open it. All open the same list.

Option A

The peek

The first three rows are already visible and fade out into the page. People can see there's a list and that it continues. It adds no new elements, just your existing rows.

Option B

Project avatar stack

A single row in the same shape as your list, led by overlapping greyscale crops of the projects. On hover they fan out slightly and get their colour back. It's a familiar pattern from avatar groups, so it hints at real work behind the click without adding weight.

Option C

Named archive row

Pure type, the quietest option. Recognisable names like Meetendo and ALFA+ create curiosity on their own, and a small count pill makes the row clearly clickable. It replaces the “Not happy yet?” line.

Small greyscale project shots in the same rounded-card style as your featured work. Hovering one brings back its colour. This is the most visual option and the strongest pull, but also the heaviest. It's a good fit if you want people to treat the archive as real work.
